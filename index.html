01 · About

I design it, build it, and ship it.

I'm a full-stack developer and web designer from Hyderabad. I like owning the whole thing: the wireframe, the interface, the API behind it, and the deploy at 2am that finally works.

My research sits in healthcare AI — multi-agent systems that handle claims, scheduling and patient records so hospital staff don't have to. Three papers so far, and a Best Presenter award at ICSDE 2026 for the latest one.

Before all that I spent four years saying yes: to hackathons, to teaching juniors, to running the club, and to co-founding Bizzapt Enterprises.
